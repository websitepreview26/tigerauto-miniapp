# Тестовые фотографии

Созданы встроенным ImageGen для демонстрации, затем уменьшены до 960 × 720 и JPEG. Копии встроены в `index.html`: отдельные файлы не нужны для запуска демо.

- `replace.jpg`: стекло Volkswagen Passat B6 с трещиной, для замены и консультации.
- `repair.jpg`: скол крупным планом, для ремонта.

Промпты:

1. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: small demo attachment photo for an auto glass service form, not marketing. Generate a realistic ordinary smartphone photo of the front windshield of a dark gray 2008 Volkswagen Passat B6 parked outdoors under overcast daylight. Front three-quarter close view showing the entire windshield, wipers and a portion of the hood. A long thin clearly visible crack crosses the lower passenger half of the windshield, indicating need for replacement. Natural reflections, understated everyday documentation photo, no people, no text, no labels, no watermark, no legible license plate. Landscape 4:3 composition.

2. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: small demo attachment photo for auto glass repair form, not marketing. Generate a realistic ordinary smartphone macro photograph of one small 8mm stone-impact bullseye chip in a car windshield with a few tiny radial fissures, situated off-center on the passenger side. Taken from outside, windshield surface and real subtle reflections visible, dashboard and dark gray hood softly out of focus. Understated everyday documentation photo in soft overcast daylight. Only one localized small chip, no long cracks. No people, no fingers, no tools, no text, no labels, no watermark. Landscape 4:3 composition.
